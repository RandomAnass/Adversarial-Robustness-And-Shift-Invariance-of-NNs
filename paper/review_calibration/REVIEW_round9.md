## Summary

The paper argues that the two opposing literatures on shift-invariance and adversarial robustness — Ge et al. (2021), where forced invariance collapses the robust radius to `O(1/√d)`, and the anti-aliasing line where more invariance helps — are two regimes of one scalar: the margin of the discriminative signal that survives projection onto the invariant subspace, divided by that feature's Lipschitz constant (`η/L`). The theory contributes: an exact identity for the invariant linear margin as the separation of the DC-projected data (Thm 1) with a finite-group generalization (Prop 1); the classical certificate `r₂ ≥ η/L` completed to a *conditional* two-sided bracket `η/L ≤ r₂ ≤ η/α`, `κ = L/α` (Prop 2); a conv+quadratic+pool = power-spectrum lemma (Lemma 1); recoveries of Ge (2021) and an interpretation placing Kamath et al. (2021) as a small-`η/L` corner; an exact radial–tangential split of the margin gradient (Prop 4) with a new achieved-margin envelope (Prop 9); orbit-rank degeneracy results (Props 5–7); and a lazy/NTK cluster theorem (Thm 2) that invariance lowers the certified Lipschitz constant. Empirically it is a capacity-matched architectural dissection on MNIST / Fashion-MNIST / CIFAR-10(-100), standard and PGD adversarial training, two threat models, scaling to PreActResNet-18 with a second exact-invariance mechanism (adaptive polyphase), with a per-sample certificate, bootstrap CIs, gradient-masking checks, and an attack-free selection demonstration. The central message: shift-consistency does **not** predict robustness and *anti*-predicts it under AT, whereas a threat-matched `η/L` predicts it; exact invariance hurts by collapsing the margin while graded anti-aliasing helps; and `η/L` is a diagnostic, not a trainable target.

**My verdict.** Honest, technically sound, unusually well-controlled, and genuinely unifying — clearly richer than the two nearest rejected analogs in the corpus. The round-9 polish is real and lands: the abstract reads better, the `τ`/`κ` confusion that a round-8 reviewer flagged is now cleanly resolved, and the envelope theorem tightens the untrainability story. But none of these touch the binding constraint: the paper's own diagnostic delivers no positive lever that beats a free, already-computed baseline (`η/L` *ties* clean accuracy as a selector and provably fails as a training target). That is the exact contribution profile the corpus rejects without a champion. It sits right at the borderline; I land marginally below the bar by default, accept-if-championed, unchanged in category from rounds 7–8 but a stronger 5 on presentation.

## Strengths

- **S1 — Reconciles a real, citable contradiction.** Placing Ge (2021) and the anti-aliasing line on one axis is a genuine conceptual contribution. The Ge recovery (`γ_inv = 1/√d`), the power-spectrum sign-blindness argument (*"The power spectrum is sign-blind ... so even the power-spectrum invariant cannot separate the dot"*), the bispectrum escape, and the Kamath "small-`η/L` corner" reading are clean and, where I checked, correct. This is more than the single-phenomenon contribution of `aS2Yl8s5OG` or the single-metric critique of `KX5hd1RhYP`.

- **S2 — The proofs I checked are correct, with no flawed step.** Thm 1 (invariance ⟹ `w ∈ span(1)`, margin = half inter-interval distance), Prop 1 (group-average projector), Lemma 1 (Parseval), Prop 4 (Euler radial floor + spherical-tangent split), Lemma 3 (scale-invariance ⟹ constant-classifier optimum), the toy model (App. A: `min (a−r)²+r²` at `r=a/2` gives radius `a/√2`, which I recomputed), the bracket (App. B), and the lazy-cluster theorem (App. B, Elesedy projection + Pythagorean identity, honestly gated to smooth activations in Remark 1) all check out. The new **envelope theorem (Prop 9)** is a correct parameter-homogeneity scaling argument: `γ_IB` is scale-invariant, `C(θ̂)=1/γ_IB`, and the bound `‖∇ₓM‖ ≤ B_i/γ_IB` follows, with the honest caveats that GD reaches a KKT point that "*need not be globally max-margin*" (Lyu-Li / Ji-Telgarsky / Vardi) and that "*The constant cannot be data-independent*" (the explicit four-point `τ→∞` construction). This is not a "theorem-is-wrong" review.

- **S3 — The `τ` vs `κ` disambiguation now reads cleanly (the round-8 fix landed).** A round-8 reviewer read the "≈26" figure as the *bracket* condition number, which would have made "`η/L` determines `r₂` up to `κ`" vacuous on the trained networks. The paper now states plainly that the "≈26" is the **envelope factor `τ`** — the slack between the gradient and its radial floor, `τ = ‖∇M‖/(M/‖x‖)` — and reserves `κ = L/α` for the bracket's looseness: *"This `τ` is the slack between the gradient and its radial floor, distinct from the bracket condition number `κ=L/α` of Proposition 2 (the certificate's looseness): `τ=1` means the gradient is purely radial, `κ=1` means the certificate `r₂=η/L` is tight."* The figure caption repeats the distinction. `κ≈1.6–2.4` is now correctly attached to the power-spectrum surrogate, `τ≈26` to the trained networks. The two objects are genuinely different (one is about gradient direction, the other about certificate tightness), and the disambiguation is now correct and consistent across body, proposition, and figure. This is a real clarity win.

- **S4 — Evaluation soundness is a real differentiator.** AutoAttack ≤ matched PGD for every arm and condition (no gradient masking, including the non-differentiable adaptive-polyphase arm); the DDN radius validated against the closed-form linear radius "*to under one percent*"; the per-sample certificate (Spearman ≈ 0.91–0.95, positive in all 36 CIFAR + CIFAR-100 models); bootstrap CIs + permutation tests; two threat models; a second exact-invariance mechanism at ResNet scale. This is exactly the rigor whose absence sank method-bearing rejects in the corpus.

- **S5 — Honest scoping, now consistent and foregrounded.** The bracket is "*under an explicit reachability condition*"; Kamath is "*an interpretation ... a self-contained restatement ... would be needed to make it a theorem*"; the 0.998 correlation is down-weighted as "*high by construction ... rather than a separate finding*"; the load-bearing claim is named as "*the dissociation*"; the no-converse fact and normalization-dependence are in §7. The corpus rewards this kind of scoping (the `aS2Y` reviewers praised it even while rejecting).

- **S6 — One genuine positive result, correctly attributed.** The graded anti-aliasing sweep gives "*+0.013 (bootstrap CI [+0.011,+0.014])*" robust-accuracy gain "*at matched clean accuracy ... to within one point (range 0.010)*," beating the aliased baseline in 39/40 cells. This is a real gain not explained by clean accuracy — but it confirms the Grabinski/Zhang recipe; the paper's contribution there is the margin-vs-Lipschitz *mechanism*. The four-intervention falsification loop (theory predicts collapse → all four penalties collapse the margin, AA flat-to-down, no masking) is clean and rare.

## Weaknesses (ranked, located, each quoted and verified)

**W1 — The diagnostic supplies no positive lever that beats a free baseline; this is the binding constraint and the polish does not move it. [§5 selection rule; §5 training; abstract]**
The decisive corpus gate is whether a paper offers something a practitioner could not get for free. Here it does not. As a *selection rule*: *"Clean accuracy is an equally good selector ($0.08$ points of regret), consistent with the margin being the common driver of \S\ref{sec:exp}; the operative point is the failure of consistency, not a margin of $\eta/L$ over clean accuracy."* As a *training target*: *"every first-order attempt to raise it collapses the margin, leaving robustness flat or worse."* So the deliverable is a *negative* operational result (do not select invariance operators by shift-consistency) plus a reconciliation and a diagnosis. **Verified, and the paper foregrounds it** — this is not me reading it uncharitably. This is the category the corpus rejects without a champion: `aS2Yl8s5OG` (exemplary presentation, two 7s, no lever over the cheap baseline → *Reject*, "no reviewer stepped forward to champion ... contribution somewhat marginal") and `KX5hd1RhYP` (correct first-of-kind negative → *Reject*, "technically limited / too narrow / workshop"). The submission is richer than both — which keeps it from falling *below* the borderline — but the tie keeps it from clearing it. Not a penalty for honesty (the honesty is S5); it is the contribution category the corpus gates on. The round-9 changes are all on the *presentation/theory-clarity* axis and leave this axis untouched.

**W2 — The restructured abstract still sells the selection rule as a positive lever and omits the clean-accuracy tie the body discloses. [Abstract]**
The abstract now opens well with the contradiction hook, but its operational sentence reads: *"Used as an attack-free rule for choosing an invariance operator, $\eta/L$ picks the most or near-most robust one, while shift-consistency picks the least."* A reader takes this as a positive lever. The decisive caveat — that clean accuracy ties `η/L` (0.08 vs 0.06 points of regret) — appears only in the body. To the authors' credit the §1 contribution bullet does carry the parenthetical "*(like clean accuracy)*," so the honest framing exists; it just has not propagated to the abstract. **Verified** (I scanned the abstract end to end: clean accuracy is never named as a selector there). This is a locatable, fixable framing inconsistency, not a fabrication, and it is the same gap flagged in rounds 7–8 — the abstract restructure improved the opening but did not close it.

**W3 — The two-sided bracket's converse arm is verified only on a surrogate, not on the models the paper studies. [Prop 2; App. B; §7]**
The `τ`/`κ` cleanup (S3) is genuine, but it also makes explicit that the bracket's upper arm `r₂ ≤ η/α` is established numerically only where `κ` is small: *"for the power-spectrum surrogate both arms are explicit (verified numerically, realized $\kappa\approx1.6$–$2.4$)."* On the trained CIFAR/ResNet models the paper reports `τ≈26` (the envelope factor) but never measures the co-Lipschitz `α`, hence never `κ`, on a trained network — so "*`η/L` determines the robust radius up to `κ`*" is demonstrated on a power-spectrum surrogate, not on the dissected models. **Verified**; the proof (App. B) is correct and the upper arm legitimately assumes a co-Lipschitz bound along a reaching path (close to assuming a descent path to the boundary, but stated as an explicit hypothesis, not hidden). This is honestly scoped (§7: "*a lower-bound certificate with no general converse*"), so it is a *strength/novelty-of-the-converse* limitation, not a correctness defect: the "conditional converse" remains a surrogate-verified add-on, and a skeptical reviewer will say the converse contributes little on the regime the paper actually cares about.

**W4 — The flagship `η/L`↔`r₂` correlation (0.998) is near-tautological, by the paper's own statement. [§5 Statistical power]**
*"The $\eta/L$–radius value is high by construction: $\eta/L$ is a first-order lower bound on $r_2$ and these models are close to locally linear, so this correlation is the across-cell face of the per-sample certificate ... rather than a separate finding."* **Verified, and admirably candid.** It means the most eye-catching number (Figure 4, Pearson 0.998) is `r₂ ≈ η/L` measured on near-linear models, not independent corroboration. The genuinely load-bearing claim is the *dissociation* (consistency carries no info; `η/L` carries it), which then rests on the smaller-grid significance of W5. A reader skimming the figures may over-read the 0.998 as the evidentiary backbone.

**W5 — The consistency anti-prediction is weak inside the capacity-matched comparison and sharpens only with range-widening. [§5 Scaling; §5 Statistical power]**
*"Across the four capacity-matched arms alone the anti-prediction is weak and not significant ($-0.43$); it sharpens to $-0.81$ only once the two low-invariance arms widen the consistency range. This is the point rather than a caveat."* **Verified.** The saturation mechanism (a strong backbone squeezes consistency into `[0.93,1.00]`) is plausible and well argued, and the 24-cell standard grid leaves consistency non-significant (`-0.33`, CI `[-0.68,+0.09]`, `p=0.11`) while the 12-cell AT grid is significant (`-0.75`, CI `[-0.92,-0.02]`, `p=0.006`). So this is "thin in places," not "unsupported" — but the dramatic `-0.81`/`-0.88` numbers depend on arm selection, and a skeptical reviewer will weigh that. No ImageNet (acknowledged, §7); the nearest accept `xDxskDUvte` was pushed to ImageNet-100 in rebuttal and complied.

**W6 — Much of the scaffolding is assembled from known ingredients; the new envelope theorem is a modest scaling corollary, and the authors say so. [§3.2–3.5, App. A–E]**
By the authors' own statement, *"The Lipschitz–margin certificate $r_2\ge\eta/L$ is classical \citep{hein2017formal,tsuzuku2018lipschitz} ... Our contribution is not the bound but the shift-invariance dissection it supports."* The bracket rests on a co-Lipschitz hypothesis; the lazy-cluster theorem on Elesedy projection + smooth activations (open for ReLU); Prop 4 is the standard radial/tangential decomposition of a 1-homogeneous gradient; the new Prop 9 is a homogeneity-scaling envelope. These are correct and honestly framed as "*modest structural contributions*" and "*a property of the solution's data geometry, not a trainable law*," but the load is carried by the empirics and the reconciliation, not by new theory. This is the `KX5hd1RhYP` pattern ("relatively obvious ... a direct consequence") — not a defect, but it caps the technical-contribution axis, and the round-9 theorem addition does not add a new lever.

**W7 (presentation, minor) — The abstract is still a single ~400-word paragraph that front-loads undefined jargon. [Abstract]**
"co-Lipschitz," "orbit-closed," "threat-matched," "self-symmetrizes," "homogeneous," "scale-invariant," "data condition number" all appear before any is defined. The restructure (opening with the helps-vs-harms contradiction) is a real improvement and the body, tables, and figures are clear; the abstract's front door remains the bottleneck. **Verified.** The `C36v8541Ns` thread shows reviewers penalize this verbosity ("I struggled a lot") but that the paper can survive it if the contribution carries through — so this is minor and survivable, not decisive.

**Minor / could-not-fully-verify**
- The bispectrum claim ("*separates `±e_j` with margin `d^{-3/2}`*") is stated without proof or experiment; plausible (odd-degree in the spectrum, hence sign-sensitive) and explicitly not load-bearing, but mark it as a claim.
- Props 1, 5–7 (group margin, orbit-rank degeneracy) have no empirical counterpart; modest theory-only extensions.
- I could not independently verify the reported bootstrap CIs or the `τ≈26` / `κ≈1.6–2.4` figures without the data and code (provided with the submission but not run here).

## Questions to authors

1. **Can `η/L` ever beat clean accuracy as a selector?** This is the whole ballgame (W1). Is there a regime where clean accuracy and robustness *decouple* (across very different capacities, under corruption / distribution shift, or on a held-out architecture) and `η/L` selects the more robust operator while clean accuracy does not? One such condition converts the diagnosis from "correlate of clean accuracy" into "tool that adds value."
2. **Held-out validation.** Can you exhibit one *unseen* architecture or task where deploying the highest-`η/L` operator yields a measured robustness gain over *both* the consistency-chosen *and* the clean-accuracy-chosen operator? The current five conditions are the same arms used for the correlations, re-read as a selection problem.
3. **The bracket on real models.** Is the co-Lipschitz `α` (hence `κ`) measurable on the trained ResNets, or only on the power-spectrum surrogate? If only the latter, Prop 2's "characterization up to `κ`" should say so in its statement, not only the appendix (W3).
4. **Smallest balanced grid.** Given the four-arm `-0.43` (n.s.) vs the widened `-0.81`, what is the smallest *balanced* grid (equal arms spanning the consistency range, not range-widened) for which the AT anti-prediction is significant (W5)?
5. **ImageNet feasibility.** Do the two laws hold on even one mid-scale dataset (e.g., ImageNet-100, as the comparator added in rebuttal)? A single positive data point would materially strengthen generality.

## Scores

- **Soundness: 3 (good).** Every proof I checked is correct, including the new envelope theorem; the empirics are unusually well-controlled (per-sample certificate, gradient-masking checks, two exact-invariance mechanisms); residual caveats (W3 surrogate-only converse, W4 by-construction correlation, W5 small grids) are honest and minor.
- **Presentation: 3 (good, up from fair).** The `τ`/`κ` disambiguation removes the one genuine technical-clarity hazard a round-8 reviewer hit; the abstract is better structured; figures and tables are clear. The remaining drag is the run-on abstract and its omission of the clean-accuracy tie (W2, W7).
- **Contribution: 2 (fair, bordering good).** A genuine reconciliation, an operationalized "consistency fails" finding, a clean untrainability result, and strong evaluation; capped by the absence of any lever from its own diagnostic that beats a free baseline (W1) and by assembled-from-known-ingredients theory (W6). Sits between `aS2Yl8s5OG` (contribution 2s, rejected) and the method-bearing accepts (contribution 3s) — closer to the former in *category*, closer to the latter in *rigor*.

## Single highest-value change

**Produce one setting where acting on `η/L` yields a measured robustness gain over *both* shift-consistency *and* clean accuracy.** Concretely: find or construct a regime where clean accuracy and robustness disagree (across capacities, under corruption / distribution shift, or on a held-out architecture/task) and show the highest-`η/L` operator is more robust than the clean-accuracy-chosen one. This is the move that separates every corpus accept (`xDxskDUvte` beats the CNN, `C36` beats RS, `M9SK` beats TRADES, `dT0eNsO2YLu` beats APS) from the rejects (`aS2Y`, `KX5`), and it would convert the paper from "rigorous diagnosis that explains a known correlate" into "a tool that adds value beyond what is free" — turning my 5 into a confident 6+. Absent it, demote the abstract's selection sentence to disclose the clean-accuracy tie (W2) and present the *dissociation* (shift-consistency is uninformative / anti-predictive; exact invariance hurts through margin collapse) unambiguously as the headline; the strongest realistic home as a diagnosis-plus-reconciliation is then TMLR or a workshop (the explicit recommendation that landed `KX5hd1RhYP`).
