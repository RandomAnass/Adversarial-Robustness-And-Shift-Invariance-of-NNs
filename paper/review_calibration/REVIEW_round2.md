# Review — "When Does Shift-Invariance Permit Adversarial Robustness? A Margin-Preservation Theory"

*Calibrated against 16+ full OpenReview threads in the adversarial / certified / Lipschitz / invariance / anti-aliasing-robustness area (NeurIPS 2025 "Bridging Symmetry and Robustness" xDxskDUvte; ICLR 2024 Lipschitz-Variance-Margin C36v8541Ns; ICLR 2025 Invariance-Regularization M9SKazbVkJ and Group-Downsampling sOte83GogU; the weak-reject and clear-reject buckets). Rating scale: ICLR 1–10 (6 = marginal accept, 5 = marginal reject, 8 = accept).*

## Summary

The paper proposes a single scalar, the margin-to-Lipschitz ratio η/L (threat-matched to the dual norm of the attack), as the axis that reconciles two opposing literatures on shift-invariance and adversarial robustness: Ge et al. (2021), who prove that full shift-invariance can collapse the robust radius to O(1/√d), and the anti-aliasing line (Zhang 2019; Grabinski 2022; Saha 2024), where making a network more shift-invariant improves robustness. The thesis is that invariance helps only when the discriminative signal survives projection onto the invariant subspace with positive margin relative to the feature's sensitivity. Contributions: (i) a structural theory — an exact identity for the invariant linear margin as the DC-projection separation (Thm 1), its finite-group generalization (Thm 2), a co-Lipschitz "two-sided bracket" η/L ≤ r₂ ≤ η/α completing the Lipschitz–margin certificate up to a condition number κ (Prop 2, App A.2), a power-spectrum span lemma (Lem 1), and recoveries of Ge and a "corrected" reconciliation of Kamath; (ii) an optimization section — orbit rank, the degeneracy of the two canonical orbit constructions, a "margin-free" result on orbit-closed data, and a lazy/NTK cluster theorem that invariance lowers the certified Lipschitz constant (Thm 4, App A.2); (iii) experiments — a training-free structural test, a capacity-matched architectural dissection on MNIST/Fashion/CIFAR-10, and PGD-AT dissections across three datasets and two threat models scaling to PreActResNet-18, with a per-sample certificate. The headline empirical claims are that threat-matched η/L predicts robust radius / AutoAttack robust accuracy (Pearson up to +0.9, up to 0.998 across cells) while shift-consistency does not and under AT anti-predicts (down to −0.9).

This is a single-author analysis/reconciliation paper, not a new-defense paper; it should be judged as a unifying-conceptual contribution plus a diagnostic empirical study.

## Strengths

1. **A clean, genuinely useful reconciliation with an operational decomposition.** The central move — splitting the robustness change into a margin term Δlog η and a Lipschitz term Δlog L (Fig. 3 / §Architectural dissection) — gives a concrete, falsifiable account of *why* the two literatures disagree: anti-aliasing helps by lowering L at roughly fixed margin, while exact cyclic invariance hurts by shrinking η. This is the kind of "places prior work on a common axis" framing the accept bucket rewards (cf. the NeurIPS-2022 Boolean-Lipschitz paper's "explains why MaxMin fails and when L∞-nets succeed"). It is the paper's real product and, to my knowledge, this specific margin-vs-Lipschitz decomposition of the shift-invariance/robustness disagreement is new.

2. **Methodologically careful empirics, above the bar of several reject-bucket papers.** Gradient-masking is checked throughout (AutoAttack ≤ matched PGD in every condition; the DDN minimum-norm radius validated against the linear closed form to <1%; AA-vs-radius agreement at small ε to mean gap 0.014). The clean-accuracy confound on the exact-cyclic arm is anticipated and partly defused with parameter-matched low-invariance arms and a partial correlation (−0.82) on the ResNet. Bootstrap CIs and permutation tests are reported on denser grids. This is materially more rigorous than, e.g., the Block-Reflector (yi3QcCGfP1) or Frequency-Pooling (SyxD7lrFPH) rejects.

3. **Honest scoping of the theory.** The paper repeatedly states what it does *not* prove: "no universal converse" for the certificate; the bare-ReLU NTK caveat (Remark 1, correctly attributing non-Lipschitz ReLU-NTK to Bietti–Mairal); the rich-regime trajectory "stays open"; and the Kamath reconciliation explicitly flagged as "an interpretation … a self-contained restatement would be needed to make it a theorem." This candor is the opposite of the overclaiming that sank papers like COCK (OwtkY3LyvC) and is the behavior the accept bucket displays.

4. **Correct proofs and reasonable hypotheses (checked).** I verified App A.2. Thm 1/2 are correct (invariance ⟺ w in the fixed subspace; max-margin of two point sets = ½·hull distance). Prop 2's two arms are valid: the lower arm is the standard certificate; the upper arm correctly observes that any continuous reaching path with co-Lipschitz rate α puts the boundary within η/α, and α ≤ L follows from the secant. Thm 4 is correct: for a shift-invariant kernel the Reynolds operator is the orthogonal projection onto the invariant subspace (Elesedy 2021), orbit-closedness makes Π_G f_unc feasible, norm-contraction gives the smaller certified Lipschitz constant, and the gap is exactly the discarded orbit-variance. The smoothness hypothesis is correctly required and honestly caveated.

5. **Scale and breadth for an analysis paper.** PreActResNet-18, two backbones for exact invariance (circular padding and adaptive polyphase, Chaman 2021), three datasets, two threat models, plus a per-sample certificate and the margin–sensitivity sign-flip observation. Concurrent work (Wang et al. 2025, the NeurIPS-2025 spotlight that is effectively the competing paper) is cited and positioned precisely as "the shift-consistency ingredient we find necessary but not sufficient."

## Weaknesses (most to least serious)

1. **The headline correlation is close to tautological, and the paper leads with it.** η/L is, by the authors' own statement, a first-order *lower bound* on r₂, and the dissected models are "close to locally linear," so the across-cell Pearson 0.998 (Fig. 1 / Fig. `cifar_etaL_vs_radius`, whose own panel title reads "+1.00") and the per-sample Spearman ≈0.91–0.97 are largely the certificate read back on itself rather than an independent empirical law. The paper concedes this ("high by construction … the across-cell face of the per-sample certificate"), but the abstract and figures still foreground these numbers. A demanding reviewer discounts them heavily and asks what is left. What is left — the *dissociation* (consistency carries no radius information, η/L carries it) — is real but far weaker than advertised: on the 24-cell standard grid shift-consistency vs radius is −0.33 with CI [−0.68, +0.09], p=0.11 (not significant), and Fig. 1's own right panel shows only −0.55. The most-quoted number is the least informative; the load-bearing number is borderline.

2. **The "shift-consistency does not predict robustness" framing risks being a straw man, and the surviving anti-prediction rides on one degenerate arm.** No one in the anti-aliasing line claims raw circular-shift-consistency predicts adversarial robustness; they claim anti-aliasing *operators* help — which the paper's own data confirm (anti-aliased ≥ standard everywhere). The strong negative correlations are specific to adversarial training *and* are dominated by the single exact-cyclic arm (perfect consistency, lowest robustness). The paper itself reports that over the four capacity-matched arms on the ResNet the anti-prediction "washes out" (−0.43, n.s.) and only reaches −0.81 once the two extra low-invariance arms widen the axis. So the headline "consistency anti-predicts (−0.9)" is carried by a hand-placed degenerate endpoint plus AT, not by a smooth trend. This needs to be stated as the primary caveat, not relegated to the ResNet paragraph, and the decomposition (Weakness-free, point 1 of Strengths) should be the lead claim instead.

3. **Individual theoretical novelty is modest; much is assembly of known machinery.** Thm 1 is, as written, a convex-hull/interval restatement and finite-group lift of Ge's DC observation; Lem 1 (conv + quadratic activation + GAP = power spectrum) is classical scattering/Parseval (Bruna–Mallat); Thm 4 is a fairly direct corollary of Elesedy-2021 projection plus an RKHS-Lipschitz bound; Lem 3 (orbit rank = #nonzero Fourier coefficients) is immediate from circulant diagonalization. The two-sided bracket (Prop 2) is conditional to the point of being near-definitional: α is defined precisely so the upper arm holds, is not given an a-priori estimator, is "exact for linear" (where it reduces to the textbook point-to-hyperplane distance), and κ ≈ 1.6–2.4 is only "verified numerically" for one power-spectrum surrogate. The paper is honest that "our contribution is not the bound," but the cumulative theoretical core is a well-organized synthesis rather than a new primitive. Against this bar, the calibration is split: the accept bucket (Frame Averaging; Boolean-Lipschitz) led with a genuinely new theorem (universality; Ω(d) depth lower bound), whereas this paper's new content is the *framing*.

4. **A headline reconciliation is downgraded to interpretation, but the abstract states it as settled.** The abstract says "the same scalar reconciles the apparent counterexample of Kamath," yet §3.4 admits this is "an interpretation," not a theorem in the framework, because the exact Kamath distribution is not re-derived. This is a (mild) claims-vs-evidence mismatch of exactly the kind reviewers penalize; either make it a theorem or soften the abstract.

5. **The optimization section is acknowledged-incomplete and partly synthetic.** The motivating question — does GD *reach* the robust invariant solution? — is left open (only the lazy/NTK regime is closed; "rich (small-init) and bare-ReLU trajectories stay open"). The orbit-rank/degeneracy results are, by the authors' own words, "modest structural contributions" that "do not close the optimization gap," and the cluster advantage (factor 1.2 rich, up to ~4 lazy) is demonstrated on synthetic orbit data only. The section reads as scaffolding for future work and could be compressed; reliance on an external, unreviewed "companion technical report" for the roadmap weakens it further.

6. **Reproducibility and statistical power.** No code/data-availability statement or link appears in the manuscript; the accept bucket increasingly treats released code as a strength (xDxskDUvte: "Code provided is clear and complete"; SuH5SdOXpe: weights released). AT results use only 2 seeds at 8 cells; the denser grids help but several key correlations are borderline (consistency-vs-AA CI [−0.92, −0.02], p=0.006; threat-matched p=0.002 with wide CIs). No ImageNet (the competing xDxskDUvte added ImageNet-100 in rebuttal; Block-Reflector was dinged for its absence). For a dissection paper this is forgivable but will be raised.

7. **Presentation: the abstract is a single ~350-word jargon-stack.** η/L, the two-sided bracket, κ, orbit-rank, margin-free, lazy/NTK, bispectrum d^{-3/2}, and self-symmetrization are all crammed into one paragraph. This is precisely the verbosity the Lipschitz-Variance-Margin reviewers repeatedly punished ("verbose," "wordy," "could be stated in a single line"). The body is also number-dense. Mitigating: the figures are clean and qualitative takeaways do exist ("invariance helps robustness only when it does not cost margin").

## Questions

1. Since η/L is a first-order lower bound on r₂ and your models are near-locally-linear, can you quantify how much of the 0.998 is non-trivial — e.g., report η/L vs r₂ after *removing* the mechanical lower-bound component, or on models deliberately pushed away from local linearity? What would falsify the η/L law as opposed to confirming the certificate?
2. Strip the exact-cyclic arm: does the consistency anti-prediction survive among only the non-degenerate arms under AT, and at what effect size? If not, please reframe the claim around the margin/Lipschitz *decomposition* rather than around "consistency anti-predicts."
3. For Prop 2, is α ever computable a priori for a real (non-linear, non-surrogate) feature map, or is it always identified post hoc? If the latter, what does the bracket buy beyond the existing one-sided certificate plus the observation κ ≥ 1?
4. Will you either (a) re-derive the Kamath distribution to make the reconciliation a theorem, or (b) reword the abstract to present it as an interpretation? As stated, the two disagree.
5. The decomposition claims anti-aliasing lowers L with margin "roughly unchanged." Is the L-reduction attributable to anti-aliasing per se, or to the accompanying clean-accuracy gain? Please add a partial correlation controlling clean accuracy for the *standard-training* dissection, mirroring the ResNet AT analysis.
6. Is code available? Without it, the per-sample certificate, DDN radii, and gradient-masking checks are hard to verify.

## Scores

- **Soundness: 3 (good).** Proofs are correct and honestly scoped; the empirical protocol (gradient-masking checks, DDN validation, partial correlations, bootstrap CIs) is careful. Held back from 4 by the partly-tautological headline correlation and the borderline significance / small-n of the genuinely load-bearing dissociation.
- **Presentation: 3 (good).** Clear golden thread, clean figures, candid limitations — but a jargon-dense single-paragraph abstract and number-heavy prose that the calibration threads explicitly penalize. A minor notation slip (Fig. concept uses "R" for the radius the body calls r₂).
- **Contribution: 3 (good).** A real, citable reconciliation and a careful diagnostic study, but assembled from known theoretical pieces with a headline that oversells a near-definitional correlation, and with the optimization story openly unfinished.

## Overall rating: 5 / 10 — marginally below the acceptance threshold (high 5; a 6 is within reach with the fixes below)

The reconciliation and the margin/Lipschitz decomposition are genuinely useful and the experiments are unusually careful for this corner of the field — clearly above the clear-reject bucket and comparable in conceptual ambition to the borderline-accepts (LVM-RS, Invariance-Regularization). What keeps it below the line today is the gap between how the results are *sold* and how much is *new and independently informative*: the most prominent number (0.998) is close to a tautology, the "consistency anti-predicts" headline leans on a single degenerate arm and on AT, the individual theorems are honest re-assemblies of known machinery, one headline reconciliation is admittedly not a theorem, and there is no code. None of these is fatal; all are addressable. As written, I expect a real panel to land at 5/5/6 — a borderline reject that a focused revision (or a strong rebuttal) could flip.

## Confidence: 4 / 5

I am confident on the robustness/Lipschitz/invariance literature and checked the App A.2 proofs and the key figures directly. I did not re-run the experiments or independently re-derive the Kamath embedding.

## Decision and top-3 fixes

**Decision: borderline reject (5), salvageable to accept (6).**

1. **Re-found the empirical story on the decomposition and the dissociation, not on 0.998.** Lead with the margin-vs-Lipschitz decomposition (anti-aliasing → lower L; exact invariance → lower η) as the result; demote the η/L-vs-r₂ correlation to "a sanity check that the certificate is tight," explicitly labeling it lower-bound-mechanical. Show the consistency anti-prediction with the exact-cyclic arm *removed*, and report effect sizes/CIs honestly so the load-bearing claim is the one that is foregrounded.
2. **Tighten the theory's framing and one claim.** Either upgrade the Kamath reconciliation to a theorem (re-derive their distribution) or reword the abstract to call it an interpretation; and reposition Prop 2 honestly as a conditional/near-definitional completion (state plainly that α is identified post hoc and that the linear case is the textbook radius), so the "two-sided bracket" is not read as a new converse. Compress the open optimization section.
3. **Release code and rewrite the abstract.** Add a code/data link (the per-sample certificate, DDN radii, and AA≤PGD checks all need it to be trusted) and replace the single 350-word jargon-paragraph with a plain-language abstract that states the qualitative takeaway first and the symbols second. Optionally add a denser-seed or one mid-scale (e.g., ImageNet-100/STL-10) AT point to lift the borderline significance.
